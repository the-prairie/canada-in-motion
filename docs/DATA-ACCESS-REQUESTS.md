Subject: Public redistribution and update access — Canadian petroleum well inventory

We are preparing Canada in Motion, a free, noncommercial public Canadian energy atlas. We would like to display your petroleum well inventory on an interactive map and offer attributed JSON/CSV downloads. Please confirm whether we may redistribute original coordinates and identifiers, normalize documented statuses, retain dated snapshots and publish future updates on GitHub Pages or an equivalent public host.

Please provide the applicable dataset licence or written permission, required attribution, the authoritative machine-readable inventory and count, a stable update endpoint, and definitions for well identity/re-entries, orphan designation, wellbore abandonment and site reclamation. Please distinguish observation dates from catalogue revision dates and identify onshore/offshore exclusions. We will retain unknown values and will not imply regulator endorsement.

Please identify any fields that must be removed from public distribution. If a general government open licence applies, please confirm it applies to this specific inventory and its downloadable derivatives.
